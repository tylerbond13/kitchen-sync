# Kitchen Sync — Aesthetic & Art Direction

> This document defines the exact visual target for Kitchen Sync. The reference image is not just inspiration; it is the style lock. Every station, UI card, cake, ingredient, wall treatment, floor tile, character, and animation should feel like it belongs inside that same ornate pastel cake-shop world.

## 1. One-Line Vision

**A maximalist storybook patisserie game board:** a jewel-box bakery rendered in soft 2.5D, with pastel frosting colors, gilded scrollwork, floral wallpaper, glowing ovens, candy-like cakes, ornate UI frames, and tiny expressive character sprites moving through a beautiful cake-shop stage.

The feeling should be:

- **Cake Mania warmth** — cozy bakery charm, readable stations, delicious cakes.
- **Overcooked clarity** — clear interaction zones, obvious tasks, fast gameplay readability.
- **Luxury dollhouse fantasy** — ornate gold trim, pearl highlights, carved furniture, floral wallpaper, tiny decorative props.
- **Candy-box UI** — rounded panels, gem corners, frosting borders, bright recipe icons, soft progress bars.

The target aesthetic is **not** plain pastel, minimal, generic mobile-cartoon, flat vector, or realistic kitchen art. It is a decorative fantasy bakery that looks edible, expensive, cozy, and playful.

---

## 2. Canon Reference Traits

The image target is defined by these traits:

1. **Pastel maximalism** — the screen is dense with beautiful detail, but all detail stays soft, rounded, and readable.
2. **Warm gold ornamentation** — nearly every object has gold framing, gold feet, gold bevels, gold scrolls, or gold edge trim.
3. **Purple-pink-mint cake-shop palette** — lavender walls and floor, pink frosting trim, mint cabinet panels, buttercream surfaces, berry accents, and warm metallic gold.
4. **2.5D orthographic game-board view** — furniture shows front faces and top surfaces at the same time; it is not flat front-facing only and not steep isometric.
5. **Rounded rectangular board frame** — the playable area sits inside a thick, rounded, gold-edged rectangle with soft purple interior shadowing.
6. **Decorative wallpaper backdrop** — floral wallpaper, frosting swags, roses, butterflies, bees, sparkles, and cake-like side borders.
7. **Toy-like station furniture** — ovens, counters, ingredient displays, sinks, delivery arch, and trash are ornate miniature bakery props, not utilitarian kitchen equipment.
8. **Jewel-like cakes and ingredients** — cakes are high-saturation focal points with glossy icing, berries, roses, chocolate, gold accents, and clear silhouettes.
9. **Integrated ornate UI** — order cards, side HUD, timers, buttons, and recipe icons share the same gold, gem, frosting, and pastel visual language as the bakery.
10. **Tiny character sprites** — characters are small, vertical, cleanly readable, softly shaded, and staged like little dolls inside the patisserie.

## 3. Palette

The world lives in a **pastel frosting palette with warm gold contrast**. Colors should be sweet, saturated enough to pop, and never muddy. The image is pastel, but not washed out: the purple, blue, mint, rose, berry, and gold accents are visibly vibrant.

| Role | Approx. Color Family | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Lavender wall | `#B58AD8`, `#C6A3E2`, `#E4BFE8` | wall gradient, floor shadows, UI background glow |
| Frosting pink | `#F7B6D4`, `#FF9FC8`, `#E87CAF` | frosting trim, hearts, roses, UI accents, rugs |
| Mint / aqua enamel | `#9ED9C6`, `#BCEFE0`, `#7FCFCB` | cabinet panels, oven doors, icing console, buttons |
| Buttercream cream | `#FFF0D6`, `#F7DFB8`, `#F3CFA0` | cake layers, counters, cards, plate interiors |
| Warm gilded gold | `#F0C15A`, `#D89B36`, `#A86A22` | outlines, feet, scrollwork, bevels, frames, UI borders |
| Berry red / ruby | `#D94D74`, `#C7355F`, `#F05A75` | strawberries, roses, urgency, premium cake details |
| Blueberry / grape | `#6A4ECF`, `#7350B7`, `#473080` | blueberries, purple shadows, rare/premium food accents |
| Chocolate | `#7A3B2E`, `#4A241F`, `#B56B48` | chocolate cakes, brownies, dark readable contrast |
| Plum ink | `#3A2440`, `#5A3664` | text, icon outlines, UI labels, deepest shadows |
| Pearl highlight | `#FFF6EA`, `#FFEAF6` | cards, plate centers, sparkle points, frosting glints |

### Palette Rules

- Use **warm cream instead of pure white**.
- Use **plum, chocolate, or aubergine instead of pure black**.
- Avoid industrial grey unless it is heavily tinted lavender, mint, cream, or gold.
- The gold should be warm and candy-like, not dull brass and not neon yellow.
- Functional food colors should pop more than furniture colors.
- Purple and blue accents should remain vibrant enough to stand out against the pink/lavender environment.

## 4. Camera, Projection & Spatial Language

The correct view is **2.5D orthographic, slightly top-down, dollhouse-like**.

### Do

- Show **front faces and top surfaces** of counters, ovens, stations, ingredient bins, and sinks.
- Keep verticals mostly straight; avoid extreme perspective convergence.
- Use a shallow top-down angle so players can see what is on counters.
- Use soft contact shadows so every prop feels placed on the board.
- Make interactable surfaces clear from the top: cutting boards, trays, bowls, plates, sinks, ingredient crates, ovens.

### Do Not

- Do not make assets purely flat front-facing with no visible top surface.
- Do not use steep isometric diamond projection.
- Do not make realistic kitchen perspective.
- Do not use harsh 3D rendering, hard cel-shading, or flat vector icons.

### Asset Orientation

For station sprites, the hero orientation should feel like it could sit naturally in the reference board: **front-facing with visible top plane**, softly shaded, and slightly dimensional. Left/right variants can be created by rotation or mirrored orientation, but they must retain the same 2.5D bakery-dollhouse construction.

## 5. World Layout & Screen Composition

The visual style is a **complete game board**, not a loose kitchen background.

### Screen Structure

- **Top row:** ornate rounded order tickets with cake thumbnail, clipped recipe name, ingredient icons, tool icons, and pastel progress bar.
- **Left rail:** vertical HUD panel with timer, coin count, objective/token display, circular buttons, star meter, coin reward panel, and action icons.
- **Center board:** the playable bakery floor inside a rounded gold border.
- **Right rail:** large ornate tool selector / action widget, matching the frosting-and-gold UI language.
- **Bottom strip:** instruction text in dark plum over the game board edge.

### Playable Board Frame

- The board is a rounded rectangle with thick **gold outline**, soft inner shadow, and lavender-pink border glow.
- Corners should be rounded and decorated subtly, not sharp or square.
- The frame should look like a gilded bakery tray or frosted cake plaque.

### Floor

The floor in the target image is **large lavender-and-pink checkerboard tile**, not herringbone parquet.

- Use alternating square tiles in pale pink and lavender.
- Add subtle glow specks and sparkles.
- Keep the tile pattern low-contrast enough that sprites remain readable.
- Contact shadows should be purple-tinted and soft.

### Wall / Backdrop

The wall is a layered confectionery backdrop:

- Purple-to-pink-to-mint pastel gradient.
- Floral wallpaper pattern with roses and vines.
- Frosting swag trim running horizontally.
- Decorative butterflies, bees, and small sparkles.
- Large cake/frosting side borders outside the board area.
- Draped frosting, rosettes, pearls, and candy-like decorations.

The wall should never be plain. It should feel like a decorated cake shop interior.

## 6. Materials & Rendering

The target rendering is **soft painted 2.5D with glossy edible highlights**.

### Core Materials

| Material | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Gold trim | beveled, warm, shiny, with darker caramel underside and pale highlight edge |
| Enamel furniture | mint/pink/lavender panels with soft gradients and gold outlines |
| Frosting | rounded, glossy, piped, with pearl highlights and soft shadows |
| Cakes | saturated, jewel-like, layered, glossy icing, visible toppings |
| Glass | pale blue/pink tint, white crescent highlights, transparent but readable |
| Metal | mostly gold or rose-gold; avoid cold steel except lightly tinted tools |
| Wood | warm caramel/buttercream, polished, never rustic brown unless chocolate-themed |
| Marble/countertops | cream, pink, or lavender with subtle veining, not grey office marble |
| UI cards | cream-pink surfaces with gold bevel, gem corners, soft drop shadow |

### Linework

- Exterior outlines are soft plum, rose-brown, or gold — never harsh black.
- Gold trim has a clear inner and outer edge, often with a darker orange-brown shadow line.
- Important silhouettes need readable outlines at small size.
- Decorative filigree can be thinner, but core object outlines must stay clean.

### Shading

- Light direction: soft top-left key light.
- Shadows: lavender/purple-tinted, soft, short, and painterly.
- Highlights: pearl-white or pale pink, especially on icing, glass, polished gold, and UI frames.
- Avoid hard rim lights, gritty texture, realistic grime, or noisy brushwork.

## 7. Ornamentation & Motifs

This style depends on recurring decorative motifs. Use them consistently.

### Signature Motifs

- Gold scrollwork and curled filigree.
- Hearts on cabinets, counters, trash can, and delivery arch.
- Roses and piped frosting rosettes.
- Pearls, beads, and gem corners.
- Frosting swags, scallops, and shell borders.
- Butterflies, bees, and tiny sparkle particles.
- Round medallions and ornate plaques.
- Cake tiers, pedestals, plates, and display stands.

### Ornamentation Rule

Functional centers stay readable; decoration lives on borders, feet, corners, side panels, and frames. Every major prop should have at least one ornamental signature: heart, rose, pearl, gold curl, gem, or frosting trim.

---

## 8. Station Design Language

Stations should look like ornate bakery furniture, not generic appliances.

### Ovens

- Twin domed ovens with turquoise/mint enamel, gold frames, carved legs, and glowing orange fire.
- Arched glass windows with warm fire gradients.
- Gold knobs, gold rivets, scrollwork, and heart details.
- Flames should be saturated orange/yellow and visibly alive.

### Ingredient / Crate Counters

- Ingredients sit in decorative bowls, trays, baskets, or glass bins.
- Berries, roses, chocolate pieces, sprinkles, almonds, and cake layers should be visually distinct by silhouette and color.
- Containers should have gold bases, pink/mint panels, and cream surfaces.

### Icing / Mixing Console

- Should feel like a candy chemistry station: glass icing bottles, brass/gold pipes, pastel liquids, nozzles, and a bowl.
- Icing bottles are transparent cylinders with pink, mint, cream, or lavender liquid.
- Pipes are ornate gold, not industrial metal.

### Cutting / Prep Station

- Cream or pink countertop with visible top plane.
- Cutting boards are warm buttercream or caramel wood.
- Knives have soft gold or rose-gold handles and plum-tinted blades.
- Prep surfaces are clean, rounded, and decorative.

### Sink / Dish Station

- Cream countertop, gold faucet, pink/mint cabinet face, soft blue-white dishes.
- Water is pale cyan with glossy highlights.
- Dish rack should be pink/lavender with gold accents.

### Delivery Station

- Center-front ornate arch with sign plaque reading “DELIVERY.”
- Gold frame, mint/pink panels, heart motif, arched doorway, and cream counter lip.
- It should read as a special service portal, not a normal counter.

### Trash / Utility Props

- Trash can is still beautiful: gold rim, cream body, pink gem/heart detail, decorative foot/base.
- Utility objects must not break the fantasy bakery language.

---

## 9. Cakes, Ingredients & Food Icons

Cakes are the highest-saturation focal points. They should look like tiny collectible jewels.

### Cake Rules

- Strong silhouette first: tiered, round, slice, roll, dome, square, or stacked.
- Clear color identity from a distance.
- Glossy icing, fruit, roses, chocolate shards, sprinkles, pearls, or gold leaf.
- Thick enough outline to read in order tickets and on the board.
- Small highlights and sparkles are encouraged.

### Ingredient Rules

- Ingredients should read as cute icons at small size.
- Use consistent three-quarter/top-visible rendering.
- Avoid photoreal textures.
- Food should be plump, glossy, and slightly exaggerated.

### Recipe Ticket Icons

- Order cards use cake thumbnails on the left and ingredient/tool icons on the right.
- Icons should be simplified versions of actual game assets.
- Tool icons are small but recognizable: knife, oven, icing, mixer, plate, etc.
- Progress bars use pastel fills with rounded ends.

---

## 10. UI & Typography

The UI must match the bakery world. It should feel like frosting plaques and jeweled bakery signage, not a generic mobile-game overlay.

### UI Shape Language

- Rounded rectangles with thick cream/pink fills.
- Gold beveled frames.
- Gemstone corner accents.
- Pearl highlights and subtle drop shadows.
- Circular buttons with gold rims and glossy pastel centers.
- Ornate medallions for special actions.

### Order Tickets

- Cream/pink card surface.
- Rounded corners.
- Top-left cake thumbnail or recipe emblem.
- Dark plum recipe text, often clipped with ellipsis when long.
- Ingredient icons arranged in compact rows.
- Tool icons beneath ingredients.
- Bottom progress bar with pastel fill and rounded cap.

### Left HUD

- Timer, coins, objective display, sound/menu/pause buttons, star meter, reward panel, and boost/action icons all share the same gold-framed candy UI treatment.
- HUD text uses dark plum or warm brown, not black.
- Buttons should be tactile, glossy, and gem-like.

### Right Tool Widget

- Large ornate circular/medallion selector.
- Gold filigree frame.
- Pastel icon petals or radial buttons.
- Center label in dark plum/brown.
- Should look like a magical cake-decorating control, not a plain settings menu.

### Typography

- Rounded, bold, playful, and highly readable.
- Dark plum/brown text.
- Slightly chunky mobile-game lettering.
- Avoid thin elegant serif fonts, harsh sans-serif UI fonts, or black system text.

---

## 11. Characters

Characters should feel like tiny expressive dolls inside the ornate bakery.

### Character Rendering

- Small vertical sprites, slightly oversized heads acceptable but not extreme chibi.
- Soft painted shading consistent with furniture.
- Clean silhouettes and readable outfits.
- Subtle contact shadow.
- Characters face into the board and should not look pasted on.

### Scale

- Characters are much smaller than major stations but large enough to read expression/outfit.
- A standing character is roughly comparable to the height of a counter face or slightly taller than the counter surface.
- Characters should not block key station affordances.

### Outfit / Identity Cues

- Use iconic clothing, hair shape, color blocking, and posture to make characters recognizable as archetypes.
- Keep details simplified enough for small mobile scale.
- Avoid realism that clashes with the storybook patisserie world.

---

## 12. Detail Density & Readability

The target image is dense, but not chaotic. The rule is **ornate borders, readable centers**.

### High Detail Areas

- UI frames.
- Furniture legs and side panels.
- Wall trim.
- Display cases.
- Cake decorations.
- Gold scrollwork.

### Low Detail Areas

- Main walkable floor tiles.
- Countertop work areas.
- Ingredient pickup zones.
- Character movement lanes.
- Text backgrounds.

### Mobile Readability Rules

- Every interactable station must read clearly at gameplay size.
- Use large color blocks first, filigree second.
- If decoration competes with gameplay icons, simplify the decoration.
- Use consistent object scale: cakes and ingredients are exaggerated, tools are simplified, stations are ornate but readable.

---

## 13. Motion & Feedback

The visual style should become more delightful in motion.

### Ambient Motion

- Oven flames flicker.
- Icing bottles bubble softly.
- Sparkles twinkle on the floor and cakes.
- Butterflies or bees may gently bob in background layers.
- Gold highlights shimmer subtly on premium UI or completed cakes.

### Gameplay Feedback

- Successful action: sparkle puff, soft glow, small bounce.
- Completed cake: jewel sparkle, frosting shine sweep, satisfying pop.
- Delivery: gold coin burst, star sparkle, tiny chime.
- Timer urgency: progress bar shifts from pastel to amber to berry red.
- Error/burn: smoke puff and warm red/orange warning, but still cute, not gritty.

## 14. Asset Generation Checklist

Every asset must pass this checklist before being accepted.

- [ ] Looks like it belongs inside the reference image.
- [ ] Uses pastel frosting colors plus warm gold trim.
- [ ] Has soft painted 2.5D shading with a visible top plane when appropriate.
- [ ] Avoids pure black, pure white, flat grey, harsh realism, and generic vector style.
- [ ] Includes ornamental gold detail without hurting gameplay readability.
- [ ] Uses soft lavender/purple contact shadow or transparent edge compatible with engine shadows.
- [ ] Maintains clear silhouette at small mobile size.
- [ ] Uses top-left soft light direction.
- [ ] Has rounded, edible, toy-like forms — no sharp industrial geometry.
- [ ] For stations: includes gold feet/base, decorated side/front panel, readable work surface, and clear interaction affordance.
- [ ] For UI: includes rounded cream/pink panel, gold bevel, dark plum text, and soft shadow.
- [ ] For food: looks glossy, delicious, saturated, and instantly identifiable.
- [ ] For characters: scale, shadow, and rendering match the board; they do not look pasted in.

---

## 15. Negative Style Rules

Avoid anything that would pull the game away from the target image:

- Minimalist flat mobile UI.
- Plain rectangular panels without gold trim.
- Industrial stainless-steel kitchen equipment.
- Harsh black outlines.
- Photorealistic food photography.
- Low-saturation beige/brown bakery realism.
- Overly clean modern app design.
- Dark moody lighting.
- Steep isometric projection.
- Pure front-facing sprites with no visible top surface.
- Pixel art.
- Hard cel-shaded cartoon art.
- Generic fantasy medieval ornamentation instead of cake-shop ornamentation.
- Sparse walls or empty backgrounds.
- Herringbone wood floor; the target floor is lavender/pink checker tile.

## 16. Master Prompt Template

Use this as the baseline for generating any new asset or scene:

> Create a polished 2.5D orthographic mobile game asset for Kitchen Sync, matching a maximalist storybook patisserie aesthetic. Use pastel lavender, frosting pink, mint enamel, buttercream cream, berry accents, and warm gilded gold. The object should look like ornate bakery furniture from a luxury cake-shop dollhouse, with rounded edible forms, gold scrollwork, heart/rose/pearl details, soft painted shading, visible top surfaces, glossy frosting-like highlights, plum-tinted outlines, and a soft top-left light direction. It must be readable at small mobile gameplay size, with clean silhouette and functional details clear. Avoid pure black, pure white, flat grey, industrial realism, harsh cel shading, flat vector style, or steep isometric perspective.

For UI assets, add:

> The UI should look like a frosted bakery plaque: rounded cream-pink panel, warm gold beveled frame, gemstone corners, pearl highlights, dark plum playful text, soft drop shadow, and candy-like iconography.

For food assets, add:

> The food should look like a tiny collectible cake jewel: glossy icing, saturated fruit or chocolate accents, clear silhouette, edible shine, small sparkle highlights, and readable ingredient identity.

For background/board art, add:

> The board should be a rounded gold-framed bakery stage with lavender-pink checkerboard floor, floral pastel wallpaper, frosting swags, roses, butterflies, bees, sparkles, and ornate cake-shop side borders.

---

## 17. Final Standard

A successful asset should make the player think:

**“This belongs in the same magical cake shop as the reference image.”**

It should be beautiful before it is animated, readable when it is small, and delightful when it moves.
