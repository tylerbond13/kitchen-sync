# Design roadmap — expert pass (July 2026)

Produced by a 4-lens design review (kitchen art direction, menu UI/color, retention loops, flow), synthesized and ranked by impact ÷ effort. Working through it top-down; check items off as they ship.

## Recommended palette

Warm three-tier system replacing the flat pastel candy palette in public/index.html :root (~lines 20-54). BASE (backgrounds): body gradient #EED7B4 → #F8ECD6 → #EFDCBE over #F2DFC0, --peach-bg #F6E7CE, nested rows #FAF1E1, card #FFFDF7 with border #EFDDC3, cream-deep #F3E4C8. INK (anchors): --ink #38203E, --ink-soft #6B4A66, --plum #4A2352, --plum-deep #331543 (app bar/code chip), card shadow rgba(74,35,82,.10). ACCENT VERBS: teaberry = advance/play — ramp #F4649B / #E14B7E (primary) / #C73364 / pressed #8F1D42, replacing all pinks #FF85BE #FF6FAE #E05090 #C03070 #FF5FA0 #FF9FC8; gold = spend/earn — #FFD98A / #E8A93C / #B47A18 (also stars, coin, progress fills, and it matches the in-game #F0C15A trim); teal = owned/on — #3FBFAE / #2A9A8C / pressed #1F7A6E. Eyebrow labels #B23B62 (fixes the ~2:1 contrast of pink caps on cream). This ties the menus to the in-game cream-wood + gold kitchen so the whole product reads as one art direction.

## Ranked roadmap

### 1. Purge the photoreal outliers (trash can first)
- **Effort:** tiny (minutes)
- **What:** One-line asset swap in public/js/assetManifest.js:262 — repoint trash_plain from hd/ks-trash-can.png (photoreal steel pedal bin, glaring bottom-left of game.png) to cake-world/stations/ks-cw-trash-can-front-v1.png with flat:true, scale ~0.92 (it is a generic cartoon bin, not cake-themed; CLAUDE.md only bars the mixer-as-stove). Same audit for decor_vase and decor_utensils: either drop them or replace with simple vector stand-ins using the renderer's existing rrC/gradient helpers.
- **Why:** Style cohesion is broken by its worst offender, not its average — one photoreal object reframes every neighboring sprite as a cutout. This is the classic pre-repaint cleanup and it is visible in every single round.

### 2. Grid-aligned floor stage: framed panel, walkway tint, wall seam
- **Effort:** small (half day)
- **What:** In public/js/isoRender.js draw(), before the renderQueue: draw a world-space rounded-rect (radius ~18) spanning the grid plus a 0.35-tile apron (all geometry from lvl.w/lvl.h, so builder boards get it free). Fill with the existing floorPattern() (isoRender.js:1017) at globalAlpha 0.16 + 'multiply' so the wood photo shows through with a grid-scale checker; stroke with the already-written-but-dead drawBoardFrame() (isoRender.js:961 — currently unreachable because drawScene early-returns at :924 under USE_IMAGE_BOARD); its gold #F0C15A/#A86A22 border ties in-round to the menu palette. Add a 14px AO gradient band along the panel's top edge as the wall/floor seam.
- **Why:** Nothing currently tells the eye where the kitchen ends and the backdrop begins — stations look scattered on open wood. A framed, faintly tiled stage gives sprites a surface at THEIR scale and makes '.' lanes read as walkways. Both helpers already exist; this is mostly un-deleting code.

### 3. Warm palette re-skin + depth nesting + three-verb buttons + plum app bar
- **Effort:** medium (1-2 days, pure CSS + hex greps)
- **What:** One PR, all in public/index.html's style block. (a) Swap :root (lines 20-54) to the recommended palette and the body background (lines 65-68) to the warm room gradient; grep-replace the ~20 hardcoded pink hexes to the teaberry ramp. (b) Depth rule: .card → #FFFDF7 with 1.5px #EFDDC3 border; interior rows (.level-row:573, .shop-row:1156, .ms-row:531, .cl-entry:548, .prog-chip:516) → nested darker tone #FAF1E1 with inset white highlight, killing pink-on-pink. (c) Button grammar: .btn-primary (302) → teaberry ramp with pressed shadow #8F1D42; .btn-secondary.on (317) → teal ramp; all spend actions gold ramp. (d) Promote .lobby-head (472) to a full-bleed plum band (#331543→#4A2352), Leave/icon buttons become translucent white-on-plum chips, .code-chip loses its own dark bg. (e) Fold in the small legibility fixes: empty stars #DCCDE8→#E3D2B4 with earned #E8A93C, milestone bars to 7px with visible #EFE0C6 track + gold-to-teaberry fill, eyebrow labels to #B23B62, locked chef portraits sepia(.45) instead of grayscale.
- **Why:** The user's #2 complaint answered in one coherent pass. Mid-tone background → light card → warm nested row gives instant scannability; color starts carrying meaning (teaberry=play, gold=spend, teal=owned); the plum bar gives every lobby screen a top anchor; and the menus finally share a palette with the cream-wood/gold kitchen.

### 4. Per-wallpaper color grade + interior light pool + vignette
- **Effort:** small (half day)
- **What:** Extend drawAtmosphere() in public/js/isoRender.js (~line 984 region): add a per-WALLPAPERS grade entry surfaced through staticState (wood {#FFD9A8, a:.08}, winter {#BFD8FF, .10}, wonka {#F3B8FF, .10}), painted device-space with 'soft-light' after sprites — one fillRect. Strengthen the vignette to ~0.20 when USE_IMAGE_BOARD, add a top band (rgba(40,16,44,.16)→transparent over top 18%) so the frosting-trim wall recedes, and convert the flat warm top light into a radial pool centered on project(w/2,h/2), radius ~max(w,h)*TILE_WIDTH*0.9.
- **Why:** Stations, characters, and wallpaper photos were generated with different white balance — that mismatch IS the 'pasted-on' read. One global grade plus a bright-center/dim-edge hierarchy is the cheapest harmonizer and makes the three themes actually feel different in-round.

### 5. Results screen becomes the retention engine: Retry / Next, near-miss, coin choreography, goal bar
- **Effort:** medium (1-2 days)
- **What:** Merged proposals, zero-to-tiny server changes. In public/js/app.js game_over handler (:1517): (a) add '↻ Retry' (re-emit start for same levelId, same path as btn-restart :1444) and 'Next level ▶' buttons beside Back; (b) use the starThresholds already in the payload (server/game.js:1095) — when gap to next star ≤20%, render a pulsing 'Only 140 from ★★★!' line and make Retry visually primary, else Next is primary; (c) choreograph coins: after stars land, tick 0→earned with SFX.tap every ~60ms and 6-8 CSS coin particles arcing into a wallet pill; (d) 'next unlock' bar — cheapest unowned upgrade from the same data renderShop (:961) iterates, rendered as '🪙 1,240/1,600 — Turbo Burners' on results, shop header, and lobby progress banner; (e) NEW CREW RECORD ribbon: store.recordLevelResult (server/store.js:173) returns {prevBest,isRecord}, threaded through rooms.js ~:335 — shimmering gold sweep when beaten, 'so close to the record' when within 10%.
- **Why:** Every round currently dead-ends in a lobby round-trip at the exact moment motivation peaks. Near-miss retry is the genre's proven loop-closer, and staged coin payoff plus a named goal bar answers 'why play again?' every single round — the core of the 'addicting upgrade system' ask.

### 6. Anchor the backdrop to the grid: draw the board image in world space
- **Effort:** large (2-3 days incl. per-wallpaper trimLine calibration)
- **What:** Root-cause fix for 'pasted-on'. applyWallpaper() (public/js/app.js:1675) currently sets the board photo as CSS cover on the game screen, so its scale and its trim/wall line land at arbitrary positions vs the 64x48 grid (in game.png the frosting trim floats ~200px above the top station row). Instead: pass staticState.wallpaper into the Renderer, register the image, and draw it on-canvas in drawScene() in world coordinates — scale so the painted floor region spans (lvl.w+4) tiles, and position each wallpaper's floor/trim boundary at world y = oy - 8 via a per-WALLPAPERS trimLine fraction (0 for pure-floor boards, ~0.30 for the floor70 TV rooms). Keep a blurred CSS copy (blur(14px) brightness(.92)) behind the canvas purely for edge bleed. Derives from lvl.w/lvl.h so every builder board self-aligns.
- **Why:** When the backdrop's floor line coincides with the grid's back edge and its grain scale is proportional to TILE_WIDTH, the photo stops being a poster and becomes the room. Every other grounding trick multiplies off this alignment.

### 7. Unified island shadows: one merged shadow mass per counter run
- **Effort:** medium (1 day for merged runs; silhouettes +1)
- **What:** drawBlock() gives every station its own ellipse (contactShadow at isoRender.js:1131), producing scalloped blob chains under counter rows. Pre-pass in draw(): scan lvl.grid for maximal horizontal runs of non-'.' tiles; per run draw ONE soft rounded-rect gradient shadow (core rgba(36,16,25,.26) → 0) spanning the run, and suppress per-tile ellipses inside runs (keep for isolated singles). Phase 2 (optional): cached black-silhouette cast shadows per station key (offscreen canvas, source-in fill, skew -0.25, alpha 0.10, one drawImage per station) to unify light direction.
- **Why:** A real illustrated counter run casts one continuous shadow. Merged masses make islands read as built-in furniture instead of individually placed PNGs — the biggest per-pixel grounding payoff after backdrop alignment.

### 8. Ambient decor system: seeded per-theme resolver, queue rug, wall props
- **Effort:** large (2-3 days), but phaseable: queue rug day 1, resolver day 2, wall props day 3
- **What:** Free the decor system from its one-level prison. CAKE_DECOR (isoRender.js:131) hardcodes gx/gy for one 11x6 board, gated at :836 to lvl.decor==='cake'. Replace with resolveDecor() in the Renderer constructor, layout-derived and seeded from mulberry32(staticState.seed) so all clients agree: runner rug on the longest '.' run in row h-2; round rug (cw_rug_round) at the largest open region's centroid; wall sconces at gx 0.15 / lvl.w-0.85 (the current 10.85 breaks any non-11-wide board); 2-4 seeded butterflies (diner/beach) or bees (cake). Ground the queue: vertical runner rug under the customer column using queueSlot() geometry (isoRender.js ~:492) + faint pad per slot — The Dude standing full-height on bare wood is the floatiest thing on screen. Reactivate the manifest's dormant wallAnchor props (assetManifest.js:361-377): theme sets diner = window+clock+photos, winter = window_winter+wreath+fireplace, beach = window_beach+surfboard+palm, anchored along the top edge behind row-0 stations, pos scaled by lvl.w/7 with 1.5-tile min spacing. Uses the existing three-lane depth system (rugs at -1e6, props depth-sorted, floats above) unchanged.
- **Why:** This is the literal ask — 'filler/background items to melt the gap'. Rugs break up the floor plane, wall props create the missing mid-ground band, and they are the only shipped assets that make Winter look wintery and Beach beachy in-round (the THEMES entries are currently copy-pasted). Six finished PNGs plus the whole animated decor set are sitting on disk doing nothing.

### 9. Ambient life: procedural steam wisps + seeded dust motes
- **Effort:** small (half day)
- **What:** Two additions to the existing fx pipeline (addFx/drawEffects, isoRender.js ~:692/:1281+). Steam: for stations with s.state==='cooking' (and sinks mid-wash), spawn a soft white radial-gradient blob every ~400ms per station (lastSteam map keyed gx,gy), rising 22px/s with sine sway, r 4→11, alpha .28→0 over 1.6s — replacing the flickering 💨 emoji for cooking (keep it for burned). Motes: 10-14 seeded 1.5-3px warm dots (rgba(255,244,220,.22)) drifting 3-6px/s over the floor panel's upper half, wrapping at edges. Pure canvas prims, ~25 extra draw calls.
- **Why:** A static composite screams 'pasted'; two or three slow-moving elements convince the eye it is one live illustration. Steam doubles as gameplay readability (cooking state visible across the room) and motes sell the lit-interior established by rank 4.

### 10. Roadmap as a journey: 4 node states, gold trail, one drawn coin everywhere
- **Effort:** small-medium (1 day)
- **What:** Roadmap block (index.html:608-653): DONE nodes get gold-cream gradient + #E2B454 border; CURRENT gets teaberry border, scale(1.06), pulsing ring keyframes, and a 'PLAY' ribbon via ::before; LOCKED drops the grayscale-emoji padlock (app.js currently renders 🔒 as the level emoji) for the level's own emoji at .35 opacity plus a small plum SVG lock via mask-image data-URI; the .level-group::before trail gets a JS-set --prog percent so completed portions fill gold behind you. Plus the currency fix: a .coin CSS class (radial-gradient gold disc with inset shading) replacing every gray 🪙 emoji in HUD, shop (:1149+), milestones, and crew stats; same treatment for locks with one masked SVG tinted #B9A58C.
- **Why:** The progression map is the genre's retention spine and right now locked nodes are gray emoji ghosts barely distinct from played ones. Done/current/locked silhouettes + a trail that visibly fills give players one unambiguous 'tap here next'. And money should look like money — the gray-moon coin emoji undermines the entire upgrade economy it denominates.

### 11. Shop as storefront + milestones that pay out
- **Effort:** medium (1-2 days incl. ~30 server lines)
- **What:** Shop block (index.html:1147-1193): .shop-buy becomes a gold price tag (#FFD98A→#E8A93C, shadow #B47A18); .shop-row.owned drops opacity:.6 for teal border #57BFA0 + #EDF8F2 bg + '✓ Taught' chip; .shop-row.locked keeps full-opacity text with only the price dimmed and the requirement line in #B23B62; group heads get a teaberry awning stripe; .can-buy re-keyed to gold with a 6s wiggle nudge. Milestones: attach coin rewards to the 12 entries in milestoneList (app.js:1062; 100-2,000, sized so 2-3 claims ≈ one 600-1,800 upgrade); server-side claim_milestone event in rooms.js validating against crew.stats/progress and crediting crew.wallet once (crew.claimedMilestones[id]); client shows a bouncing 'Claim 🪙250' button + confetti, and the 'See all milestones' link (:1052) gets a claimable-count badge dot.
- **Why:** Owned/locked rows distinguished only by opacity ghosting read as 'broken', not 'progression' — full-opacity locked rows with readable requirements advertise the tree players grind toward. Milestone claims convert a dead checklist into a reward dispenser and bridge new crews across the brutal 0→600-coin gap to their first purchase (visible in the screenshots).

### 12. Flow ceremony pass: transitions, tap acknowledgment, round-start ritual, round-end crescendo
- **Effort:** medium overall (Phase A hours; B and C ~1 day each)
- **What:** Phased, all client. Phase A (hours): .screen.active gets a screen-in animation (opacity/translateY(14px)/scale(.985), .28s) behind prefers-reduced-motion, + SFX.whoosh in show() (app.js:102); delegated pointerdown ripple on .btn/.level-node/.chip, :active scale(.96), 120ms delayed show() so taps are visibly acknowledged. Phase B: round-start ritual — on start_game emit (app.js:892) overlay a level title card (emoji, name, star thresholds — data already on the level object), then a server-authoritative 3·2·1·COOK! countdown (server/game.js: phase='starting' with countdown, existing phase!=='playing' guards at :243/:350/:365 already block input; ship countdown in the snapshot) with scale-pop numbers, beeps, vibrate; use the ~3s to run GFX.preloadFor(staticState) so sprites never pop in. Phase C: round-end — t≤10 woodblock ticks + breathing red inset vignette + KSMusic.duck(); at game_over, TIME'S UP stamp over the frozen kitchen (~900ms), circular clip-path wipe to cream, then the rank-5 results choreography with staggered reveals, gated Continue, and mid-round '⭐ STAR SECURED' banners when state.score crosses a threshold (app.js:1360-1376 already tracks thresholds).
- **Why:** home→lobby→game→results are four hard cuts — the single cheapest tell that this is a web page, not a game. An authored chain (title card → countdown → play → crescendo → stamp → wipe → staged results) with acknowledged taps is 90% of what 'feels expensive' means on a phone, and the crescendo manufactures the adrenaline spike that feeds rank 5's retry prompt.
