How to Play 🍳

You and your crew share one kitchen. Fill the order tickets at the top before they expire!

👆
Tap to do everything.

Tap any station and your chef walks over and does the right thing. Tap open floor just to move.

📦🔪
Grab & chop.

Tap a crate to grab an ingredient, then tap a cutting board. The board keeps chopping while you move on to the next job. Chopped food wears a little 🔪 badge — and stays chopped forever. (Crews that buy the 🤖 Auto-Chopper can flip a switch in-game for faster chopping.)

🍳🔥
Cook — but don't walk too far.

Pans, pots and ovens cook on their own. ✅ means ready. Leave it too long and it burns — trash it 🗑️ and start over.

🍽️🛎️
Plate & serve.

Grab a plate from the stack (or tap the stack while holding food). Plates and ingredients combine either way around — on counters, boards, or straight from the pot. Deliver finished plates to the green window.

🫧🍽️
Wash the dishes!

From level 2 on, plates are limited — served plates come back dirty at the sink. Tap the sink to scrub one dish back into the stack, then tap again for each next dish. The rack shows clean plates; the sink shows how many are still dirty. No clean plates, no serving!

⏱️⭐
Fast food, big tips.

Serve before a ticket runs out for tips, and chain deliveries for a 🔥 combo. Beat the score goals to earn up to 3 stars and unlock the next level.

🧑‍🍳🤝
Cook together.

Split the jobs — one chops, one cooks, one runs plates. Anyone can pause. Your kitchen code keeps your stars forever, so the crew can come back any time.
